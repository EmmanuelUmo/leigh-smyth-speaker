About Leigh

SHE DOESN’T JUST TALK ABOUT CHANGE. SHE’S BUILT IT.

Leigh Smyth FRSA brings two decades of experience making inclusion and social impact work in practice across business, government and the charity sector.

She is Founder and CEO of ImpactMatch, Co-Founder of ActionAble and a former Responsible Transformation Director at Lloyds Banking Group. Her work connects leadership, inclusion and technology with the programmes and partnerships needed to make change tangible.

From national digital skills programmes to disability and Deaf equity, Leigh brings the perspective of someone who has assembled coalitions, led delivery and seen what it takes for a good idea to reach people.

Seeing the gaps others miss

Leigh was the first in her family to attend university, working three jobs to fund her studies while navigating then-undiagnosed dyslexia. It shaped the way she works: looking for people a system has overlooked and involving them in what comes next.

Short biography +

Leigh Smyth FRSA is the Founder of ImpactMatch and a pioneer in digital inclusion, social impact and responsible transformation. As the UK’s first Responsible Transformation Director at Lloyds Banking Group, she created the Digital Skills Academy that has trained over one million people for free, and the Consumer Digital Index, still the UK’s largest measure of digital capability. She helped establish the Digital Poverty Alliance, advised the United Nations on financial inclusion, and co-founded ActionAble with Sara Weller CBE to drive workplace disability inclusion. An award-winning international speaker, chair and impact judge, Leigh turns purpose into measurable impact.

Medium biography +

Leigh Smyth FRSA is the Founder and CEO of ImpactMatch and one of the UK’s leading voices on inclusion, social impact and responsible transformation. Her career has been defined by a single idea: that inclusion is not a charity problem to be managed, but a systems problem to be solved by business, government and communities together.

Leigh introduced the UK’s first children’s technology range through retail, then helped design the Government’s Home Access Programme, whose pilot achieved an exceptional 91% take-up and led to a national rollout reaching 267,000 low-income households. As Managing Director of Race Online 2012, she worked alongside Baroness Martha Lane Fox to take digital inclusion to the heart of Downing Street and build the National Digital Champion model still in use today.

At Lloyds Banking Group she became the UK’s first Responsible Transformation Director, creating the Digital Skills Academy (over one million people trained for free), the Consumer Digital Index (the UK’s benchmark for digital capability for a decade) and the first FTSE accessibility accreditation, achieved with AbilityNet. She later advised the United Nations Environment Programme Finance Initiative on global standards for financial inclusion.

Today Leigh runs ImpactMatch, co-leads the ActionAble disability-inclusion campaign with Sara Weller CBE, and serves as a trustee of AbilityNet. She is an award-winning keynote speaker, conference chair and awards judge whose work sits at the intersection of impact, innovation and inclusion.
