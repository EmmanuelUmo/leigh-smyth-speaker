# Launch checklist

- Confirm the Zero Project award status and year. The speaker pack alternates between a 2026 finalist reference and a 2027 shortlisted initiative, and also mentions a Zero Project Award. This claim is intentionally absent from the public site.
- Supply approved media and podcast URLs before adding playable episodes or outbound links.
- Supply approved high-resolution headshots and publicity images for organiser downloads. Current site photography is extracted from the supplied speaker pack.
- Connect a booking-form delivery service if direct form submission is required. The current form opens the visitor's email app with the entered details and does not claim to have sent the enquiry.
- Confirm biographical claims and testimonials with Leigh before making the site public. The short and medium biographies and selected exact testimonial excerpts come from the supplied speaker pack.
- Consider a compressed speaker pack PDF for faster downloads; the supplied original is approximately 21 MB.
